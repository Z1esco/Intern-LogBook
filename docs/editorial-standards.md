# Editorial and confidentiality standards

[← Main dashboard](../README.md)

## Accuracy first

Every daily entry must match the intern's account of the day. Describe learning as learning and distinguish hands-on activity from theoretical study. Do not claim a lab was solved, a mitigation was successful, a vulnerability was confirmed, or a final report was approved without evidence from the intern.

## Client confidentiality

Avoid client names, project identifiers, IP addresses, URLs/hostnames, network diagrams, test accounts, screenshots, production logs, detailed exploit payloads, and copies of deliverables. This repository is public; treat every commit and its history as externally accessible. Do not disclose confidential engagement information or nonpublic internal procedures.

## University logbook structure

Each entry follows the UniKL form, in this exact order:

1. **Title:** Specific, succinct scope of the day.
2. **Descriptions of Jobs/Tasks/Project:** What work or learning was assigned or undertaken, and the objective.
3. **Activities/Work Produced:** The performed actions, tools actually used, and any tangible outputs.
4. **Comments/Conclusion:** What was learned and the significance or next area to improve.

The three explanatory fields are **single paragraphs** without bullet lists. Use plain English and explain abbreviations at first use when helpful.

## Update checklist

- Confirm the calendar date, weekday, and day sequence.
- Confirm that activities and outcomes come from the intern's notes.
- Add one new dated entry under the correct month.
- Update root/year/month indexes and navigation links.
- Run the repository validator.
- Avoid describing work that was not performed, including unspecified tool use.

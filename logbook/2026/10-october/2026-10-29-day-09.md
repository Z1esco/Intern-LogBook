## 🏷️ Title

Web Application Vulnerability Retesting and Mitigation Validation

## 📋 Descriptions of Jobs/Tasks/Project

Continued web application penetration testing retesting to validate remediation measures for previously identified medium- and low-severity vulnerabilities.

## 🛠️ Activities/Work Produced

Retested and reviewed security findings involving Stored XSS, file upload, outdated libraries, CSP, input validation, session management, and information disclosure. Documented the reported statuses of 14 findings, comprising eight CLOSED, three OPEN, and three informational findings marked N/A.

## 💡 Comments/Conclusion

Gained further experience in vulnerability validation, remediation verification, and security reporting. Improved understanding of identifying unresolved security issues and documenting retesting outcomes accurately.

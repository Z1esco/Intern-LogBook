## 🏷️ Title

Consolidation of VAPT Assessment Reports and Practical Cybersecurity Training

## 📋 Descriptions of Jobs/Tasks/Project

Continued consolidating eight draft Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) reports into a single comprehensive report for a client, covering IPT, INPT, MPOS thick client, cloud, and web application assessments. Additionally, conducted self-directed practical cybersecurity training through Hack The Box (HTB).

## 🛠️ Activities/Work Produced

Worked on combining the eight draft VAPT reports into one consolidated document, organizing assessment findings and report content from different security testing scopes to maintain consistency and readability. Continued developing practical cybersecurity skills by participating in hands-on security challenges and exercises on the Hack The Box platform.

## 💡 Comments/Conclusion

Gained further experience in consolidating technical security assessment reports involving multiple testing environments and improved understanding of professional VAPT documentation requirements. Strengthened practical cybersecurity knowledge and problem-solving skills through independent hands-on training using Hack The Box.

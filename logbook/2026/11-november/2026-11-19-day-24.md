## 🏷️ Title

Findings Validation, Exploitation Testing, and Report Generation

## 📋 Descriptions of Jobs/Tasks/Project

Continued the Infrastructure Penetration Testing (IPT) assessment and the web application and network penetration testing assessment. The focus was on validating all identified findings, carrying out exploitation testing to confirm their impact, and generating a report to document the assessment results.

## 🛠️ Activities/Work Produced

Validated all findings identified during the assessments, then performed exploitation testing using Piranha, Burp Suite, and Nessus to further examine the identified issues. Following the validation and exploitation work, generated a report to document the findings for the assessments.

## 💡 Comments/Conclusion

Gained practical experience in moving from scanning and validation to exploitation testing and reporting within an assessment. This activity reinforced the importance of confirming findings before documenting them, so that the generated report reflects verified issues and supports accurate remediation guidance.

## 🏷️ Title

Continued Infrastructure Penetration Testing and New Web Application and Network Penetration Testing

## 📋 Descriptions of Jobs/Tasks/Project

Continued the Infrastructure Penetration Testing (IPT) assessment carried over from the previous week and began a new assessment covering both web application and network penetration testing. The objective was to identify potential security weaknesses across all in-scope IP addresses and URLs through vulnerability scanning, and then to validate the scan results to determine which reported issues required further attention.

## 🛠️ Activities/Work Produced

Performed vulnerability scans using Nessus against every in-scope IP address and URL defined for the assessments. After the scans were completed, used Burp Suite to manually validate the reported findings and to check the identified vulnerabilities against the actual behaviour of the web applications. This manual verification step was carried out to confirm the relevance of the scan results and to support accurate documentation of the findings for the assessment report.

## 💡 Comments/Conclusion

Gained further practical experience in managing two assessments in parallel and in combining automated vulnerability scanning with manual validation. This activity reinforced that scanner output should not be reported without verification, as manual checks with tools such as Burp Suite help confirm genuine issues and reduce false positives before findings are documented.

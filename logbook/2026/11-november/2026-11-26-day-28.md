## 🏷️ Title

IPT Proof-of-Concept Testing and True Positive Verification

## 📋 Descriptions of Jobs/Tasks/Project

Continued Proof-of-Concept (PoC) testing for 76 findings in the Infrastructure Penetration Testing (IPT) assessment. The tasks were to test the findings, mostly at network level, to verify that each one was a true positive, and to update the report.

## 🛠️ Activities/Work Produced

Carried out PoC testing for the medium, low and informational findings, mostly at network level. Verified whether each finding was a true positive and recorded the results in the report.

## 💡 Comments/Conclusion

Gained practical experience in validating scan results manually instead of relying on scanner output alone, which helps remove false positives before they reach the report. Keeping the PoC evidence and the report in step showed the need for accurate and consistent documentation.

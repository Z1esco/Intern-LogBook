## 🏷️ Title

Infrastructure Penetration Testing and Draft Report Preparation

## 📋 Descriptions of Jobs/Tasks/Project

Continued the Infrastructure Penetration Testing (IPT) assessment by reviewing and analysing the results obtained from the vulnerability scanning activities. The task also involved preparing a draft penetration testing report to document all identified findings, including their relevant technical details and assessment observations, for further review.

## 🛠️ Activities/Work Produced

Continued reviewing the Nessus scan results and assessing the identified vulnerabilities within the client infrastructure. Organised the findings and prepared a draft IPT report to consolidate the identified security issues into a structured document. The draft report was prepared to support the documentation and review process before finalisation.

## 💡 Comments/Conclusion

Further developed practical experience in infrastructure security assessment, vulnerability analysis, and technical report preparation. This activity reinforced the importance of accurately documenting scan results and presenting security findings in a clear and structured manner for subsequent review.

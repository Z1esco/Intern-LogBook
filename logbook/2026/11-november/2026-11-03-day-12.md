## 🏷️ Title

Practical Penetration Testing Training Using Hack The Box

## 📋 Descriptions of Jobs/Tasks/Project

Conducted hands-on penetration testing practice using the Appointment, Sequel, and Crocodile machines on the Hack The Box (HTB) platform. The exercises focused on strengthening practical security assessment skills, improving familiarity with different challenge environments, and applying a structured approach to solving penetration testing tasks.

## 🛠️ Activities/Work Produced

Worked through the three HTB lab environments by reviewing the information available in each challenge, examining relevant observations, and applying problem-solving techniques to determine the next steps. The practice provided further exposure to different assessment scenarios and reinforced the importance of systematic information gathering and analysis.

## 💡 Comments/Conclusion

Continued developing practical penetration testing knowledge through hands-on exercises. The session helped reinforce structured assessment methods and highlighted areas for further practice.

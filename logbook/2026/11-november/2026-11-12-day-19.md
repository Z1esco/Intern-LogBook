## 🏷️ Title

Infrastructure Penetration Testing and Nessus Vulnerability Scanning

## 📋 Descriptions of Jobs/Tasks/Project

Participated in an Infrastructure Penetration Testing (IPT) assessment for a client by remotely accessing the assessment environment through AnyDesk. The task involved preparing the vulnerability assessment environment, configuring Nessus, and initiating vulnerability scans to identify potential security weaknesses across the in-scope infrastructure.

## 🛠️ Activities/Work Produced

Established a remote connection to the client assessment environment using AnyDesk and proceeded with the setup and configuration of Nessus. Prepared the necessary scanning settings and initiated vulnerability scans against the designated infrastructure targets. The scanning activity was conducted to gather information about detected vulnerabilities, exposed services, and potential security issues for further analysis and reporting.

## 💡 Comments/Conclusion

Gained practical experience in conducting infrastructure vulnerability assessments remotely and preparing Nessus for scanning activities. The scan results provided information for further review, validation, and documentation as part of the infrastructure penetration testing process.
